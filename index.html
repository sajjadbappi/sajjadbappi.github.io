I am a graduate student at The Ohio State University interested in microbial ecology and evolution, with a focus on microbial diversity, environmental adaptation, biogeochemical cycling, and responses to environmental contaminants. I aim to combine experimental and computational approaches to study how microorganisms interact with and respond to their environments.
