Environmental microbiology · Microbial metabolites

Biosurfactant Production and Characterization by Environmental Bacteria →

This workflow investigates environmental bacteria as potential producers of biosurfactants—microbial compounds that can change the surface or interfacial behavior of liquids. Bacterial isolates are first recovered and screened for surface-active activity. Promising isolates are then cultivated for production, and the recovered material is examined to better understand its properties. Together, these steps help distinguish an initial positive screening result from more convincing evidence of biosurfactant production.

Based on Zobaer et al., International Journal of Pharmaceutical Sciences and Research (2024).

Rhizosphere microbiology · Plant–microbe interactions

Plant Growth-Promoting Traits and Abiotic Stress Resistance in Rhizobacteria →

This approach examines bacteria associated with plant roots for two complementary characteristics: traits that may support plant growth and the ability to tolerate challenging environmental conditions. After isolation from rhizosphere samples, bacterial candidates are evaluated using laboratory assays for plant growth-promoting potential and abiotic-stress responses. Comparing these results helps identify promising isolates for further plant-based testing, while recognizing that laboratory performance alone does not establish benefits under field conditions.

Based on Chowdhury et al., Current Applied Science and Technology (2024).
