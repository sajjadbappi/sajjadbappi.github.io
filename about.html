About Me

My academic journey, experiences, and interests beyond research.

My journey

I am a PhD student in Earth Sciences at The Ohio State University, with an academic background in Biochemistry and Molecular Biology from the University of Chittagong, Bangladesh.

My research experience has introduced me to different areas of microbiology, from laboratory experiments to computational analyses. These experiences have helped shape my scientific interests and encouraged me to explore new research questions.

Originally from Chattogram, Bangladesh, my academic journey brought me to the United States, first to El Paso, Texas, and then to Columbus, Ohio. Each stage has offered new opportunities to learn, grow, and develop as a researcher.

Beyond the laboratory

Words, photographs & everyday observations

I enjoy creative writing, storytelling, and photography. These interests offer different ways to notice the details of landscapes, people, and everyday life.

Science and art have something in common: both begin with paying attention.
