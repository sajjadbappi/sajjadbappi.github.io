Research approach

The workflow connects rhizobacterial isolation with screening for plant growth-promoting characteristics and resistance to abiotic stresses. The original publication provides the specific tests and conditions.

Interpreting the method

In vitro plant growth-promoting traits and stress tolerance are indicators of potential utility, not proof of improved plant growth under field conditions.
